# Locations

### The Registry Office
Government building where legal document cylinders are stored. [[characters#Kalen|Kalen]] infiltrated it disguised as an old woman; when his disguise ran out he killed two guards and caused a massive explosion, kicking off the campaign's events.

### Ilshiar district
[[factions#The Ashen (cenizos)|The Ashen]] (*cenizo*) quarter of the city. Culturally distinct from the human population; its residents fear and avoid "[[locations#The Path of the Veil (El Camino del Velo)|The Path of the Veil]]" (see [factions.md](factions.md) / plot threads for [[locations#The Arch|The Arch]]).
Site of the plaza confrontation with [[characters#Mole|Mole]].
As of session 2026-07-14, the entire district is legally (if precariously) owned by "[[factions#The Casino|the Casino]]," via a land-cession cylinder [[characters#Naut|Naut]] recovered from Daef's body.

### The Ilshiar plaza
Central plaza in the [[locations#Ilshiar district|Ilshiar district]]. Site of the hostage standoff and the full-scale brawl against [[characters#Mole|Mole]], including his gravity vortex that killed dozens of civilians. A canal runs through or alongside it.

### The spice market
Marketplace where the party gathered intel after the explosion. Location of vendors [[characters#Luis|Luis]] and [[characters#Victor|Victor]].

### The seaside cave hideout
[[characters#Kalen|Kalen]] and [[characters#Erios|Erios]]'s hideout, used to examine the stolen registry cylinders and where Daef warned them about the hostage situation.
Also leads to a stretch of beach outside the city walls, per Kalen - a route he'd never actually followed all the way out until the party used it to reach the sea in session 2026-07-14.

### The sewers
A cramped, filth-choked tunnel network beneath the city, reachable through Ilshiar outhouses and surfacing near a commercial district outside Ilshiar territory. [[characters#Romina|Romina]] crawled through it for hours to escape the guards' house-to-house search (session 2026-07-14).

### Bern's tavern
A tavern in a commercial district outside Ilshiar territory, run by [[characters#Bern|Bern]] and his wife [[characters#Tori|Tori]]. Has a mud pig pen out back. Sheltered [[characters#Romina|Romina]] after her escape through the sewers.

### The Witch's Arm (*El Brazo de Bruja*)
A restaurant near [[locations#The docks|the docks]]. [[characters#Kenshin|Kenshin]] and [[characters#Cael|Cael]] stalled here over breakfast while shaking an unwanted guard escort, bribing the waiter [[characters#Lucas|Lucas]] to arrange a distraction (session 2026-07-14).

### The docks
Where the party plans to reconvene and board *[[locations#The Aurora|The Aurora]]* to escape the city by sea, with land routes being sealed by [[characters#Sergeant Holt|Sergeant Holt]] (session 2026-07-14).
Site of a chaotic reunion: [[characters#Romina|Romina]] turned up unconscious near a fruit cart, a vendor tried to scam [[characters#Cael|Cael]], and the party hid below deck through a guard search of the ship before it sailed out past the harbor's closing chains (session 2026-07-28).

### The Arch
An ancient portal structure. The King has secretly ordered its reconstruction, per a royal decree found among the stolen registry cylinders. Leads to "[[locations#The Path of the Veil (El Camino del Velo)|The Path of the Veil]]."
Location not yet specified beyond "being reconstructed" - purpose and current whereabouts still an open thread.

### The Path of the Veil (*El Camino del Velo*)
A dangerous plane reached via [[locations#The Arch|The Arch]]. Feared and culturally avoided by the Ilshiar/Ashen people. Nothing else about it is known yet.

### The Aurora
[[characters#Victor|Victor]]'s ship, marked with a red flower insignia. Its cargo/operations are unknown - possibly smuggling given Victor's secretive, monocled demeanor.
Confirmed to smuggle people as well as goods; has a concealed hold reached through a hatch in the mast, and is crewed not by living sailors but by a handful of translucent ghost sailors Victor summons by flute. Now sailing toward [[locations#Ilvana|Ilvana]] with the full party aboard (session 2026-07-28).
Its ghost crew saw the party off with unexpected warmth at [[locations#The Bay of Loose Knots (Bahía de los Nudos Sueltos)|the Bay of Loose Knots]], one of them lamenting that no one would ever again watch [[characters#Kalen|Kalen]] sweep the deck the way he once had, before the ship sailed off for good (session 2026-08-18).

### Ilvana
An inland city, reachable from the coast by a roughly fifteen-day voyage. New destination the party redirected [[characters#Victor|Victor]] toward, since [[locations#The Arch|the Arch]] proved too far inland to reach without cutting back through the closed city (session 2026-07-28).
By reputation (per Victor, session 2026-08-11), a place of unsettling perfection - no one argues, steals, or is asked twice, and everyone performs their function without oversight. Reaching it from [[locations#The Kingdom of Doria|Doria]] requires going through the heretical Incompletes rather than the ruling Weavers.
The party arrived in person (session 2026-08-18): a colossal white tower ringed by identical triangular buildings, entered through Reader-guarded stone arches after a ritualized interrogation. No crime, weapons, beggars, or disorder of any kind was visible anywhere in the city; ruled by an unseen "[[characters#The Tejedora (the Weaver)|Tejedora]]" (see characters.md).
Beneath the tower lies a colossal circular hall lined with thousands of identical chairs, each holding a captive bound and cocooned in living gold thread - apparently where mismatched "threads" are processed and stored. Above it, the tower's upper floors hold a trapped puzzle room floored in color-coded plates (tied to the city's ranks of robes) and flanked by murals of a golden-robed woman associated with the Tejedora, guarding a room where three *[[factions#The Weavers|tejedores]]* were found having lunch (session 2026-08-25).
That dining room now lies scorched after the party's fight there; around it are a training hall where *tejedores* drill on straw dummies, a small sitting room tended by an oblivious old janitor, and the tower's artifact vault (see below) (session 2026-09-08).
Further up, past a trap-locked bedroom stocked with red-and-white robes, lie a room of symbol-marked pillars opened by threading a golden cord in the order of [[characters#Caelan Fray|Caelan Fray]]'s poem, a vaulted hall of statues and paintings, and a long spiral staircase to [[locations#The chamber of Destiny|the chamber of Destiny]] at the tower's summit (see below). Per the janitor, the tower also holds seventy-five *vaults of soul-weavings*, where cocooned threads are stored after extraction (session 2026-09-23).

### The chamber of Destiny
The seamless circular chamber at the summit of [[locations#Ilvana|Ilvana]]'s tower. A raised, three-pointed golden knot carved into the floor - the symbol of *Destiny* - glows dimly at its center, where the girl [[characters#Emma|Emma]] kneels as arm-thick braided golden threads flow from her out through small windows, splitting until they vanish, taut, over the whole city. [[characters#Solene Brey|Solene Brey]] keeps a carpet, golden glass table, and armchair to one side. [[characters#Naut|Naut]] says the room is pure Destiny and would leave him useless outside the pendant. Site of the party's ongoing fight with Solene; the armchair and the wall behind it were wrecked by [[characters#Kalen|Kalen]]'s barrage (session 2026-09-23).
By session 2026-10-07 the sofa is broken and the wall riddled; Emma has stopped weaving and her threads are fading, and a final flash left every figure in the room looking identical to [[characters#Kenshin|Kenshin]].

### The vault of closed consultations
A huge storeroom in [[locations#Ilvana|Ilvana]]'s tower of boxes, sacks, and barrels on numbered metal racks, catalogued in a ledger titled *Registro de objetos de consulta cerrada*. Holds artifacts confiscated, deposited by the tower's "Architects," or simply unexplained. Lifting the ledger triggers a ray-firing statue. Thoroughly looted by the party (session 2026-09-08); a large *false-reading mirror* was left behind.

### Posada la Costura Justa ("The Fair Seam")
A boarding house inside [[locations#Ilvana|Ilvana]] and the first building the party found with any real decoration. Run by the innkeeper [[characters#Reselvain|Reselvain]] (see characters.md), with a chef, [[characters#Maren|Maren]], working the kitchen.
The broke party traded a day's labor here - waiting tables, tending bar, and stocking the pantry - for a room and three meals, before [[characters#Fabio|Fabio]] and then the others were intercepted and taken by Readers and *[[factions#The Weavers|tejedores]]* while running an errand for the inn (session 2026-08-25).

### The Kingdom of Doria
A purely human kingdom on the coast, hosting no other Valkarian races. Ruled by a fused religion-and-government called [[factions#The Weavers|the Weavers]] (see factions.md), whose agents, the Readers, judge outsiders by sight rather than papers, deciding whether a stranger fits into what they call "the Weave" (*la trama*).
The party was delivered here after [[factions#The Bielar|the Bielar]] granted *[[locations#The Aurora|The Aurora]]* a magical current; [[characters#Victor|Victor]] refused to enter the kingdom himself, dropping the party instead at [[locations#The Bay of Loose Knots (Bahía de los Nudos Sueltos)|the Bay of Loose Knots]] (session 2026-08-11).
The overland route from the coast passes a line of numbered waymarker stones counting down toward [[locations#Ilvana|Ilvana]], and grows steadily more unnervingly perfect - flawless fruit, birdsong timed like a rehearsed score - the closer it gets to the city (session 2026-08-18).

### The Bay of Loose Knots (*Bahía de los Nudos Sueltos*)
A remote, gull-quiet inlet beneath towering cliffs on the coast of [[locations#The Kingdom of Doria|Doria]], where [[characters#Victor|Victor]] dropped the party rather than risk entering the kingdom proper before sailing off for good (session 2026-08-11).

### Siltara
A place devastated by the [[factions#The Aeonics|Aeonic]] being [[characters#Nexus Vorath|Nexus Vorath]] in a past incarnation of the party's lives, per a shared dream revelation. Sat directly above [[factions#The Dunshal|the Dunshal]] city of [[locations#Belsharun|Belsharun]]. Nothing else about it is known yet (session 2026-07-28).

### Belsharun
A Dunshal city, apparently the party's origin point in a past incarnation, destroyed or badly damaged by [[characters#Nexus Vorath|Nexus Vorath]]. Site of a ritual in which some of the party were captured by an [[factions#The Aeonics|Aeonic]], leading to [[characters#Kalen|Kalen]]'s pact never to return. Known only through a shared dream revelation, not yet visited in the present timeline (session 2026-07-28).
