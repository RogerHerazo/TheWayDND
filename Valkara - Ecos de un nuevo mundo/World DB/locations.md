# Locations

### The Registry Office
Government building where legal document cylinders are stored. Kalen infiltrated it disguised as an old woman; when his disguise ran out he killed two guards and caused a massive explosion, kicking off the campaign's events.

### Ilshiar district
The Ashen (*cenizo*) quarter of the city. Culturally distinct from the human population; its residents fear and avoid "The Path of the Veil" (see [factions.md](factions.md) / plot threads for The Arch).
Site of the plaza confrontation with Mole.
As of session 2026-07-14, the entire district is legally (if precariously) owned by "the Casino," via a land-cession cylinder Naut recovered from Daef's body.

### The Ilshiar plaza
Central plaza in the Ilshiar district. Site of the hostage standoff and the full-scale brawl against Mole, including his gravity vortex that killed dozens of civilians. A canal runs through or alongside it.

### The spice market
Marketplace where the party gathered intel after the explosion. Location of vendors Luis and Victor.

### The seaside cave hideout
Kalen and Erios's hideout, used to examine the stolen registry cylinders and where Daef warned them about the hostage situation.
Also leads to a stretch of beach outside the city walls, per Kalen - a route he'd never actually followed all the way out until the party used it to reach the sea in session 2026-07-14.

### The sewers
A cramped, filth-choked tunnel network beneath the city, reachable through Ilshiar outhouses and surfacing near a commercial district outside Ilshiar territory. Romina crawled through it for hours to escape the guards' house-to-house search (session 2026-07-14).

### Bern's tavern
A tavern in a commercial district outside Ilshiar territory, run by Bern and his wife Tori. Has a mud pig pen out back. Sheltered Romina after her escape through the sewers.

### The Witch's Arm (*El Brazo de Bruja*)
A restaurant near the docks. Kenshin and Cael stalled here over breakfast while shaking an unwanted guard escort, bribing the waiter Lucas to arrange a distraction (session 2026-07-14).

### The docks
Where the party plans to reconvene and board *The Aurora* to escape the city by sea, with land routes being sealed by Sergeant Holt (session 2026-07-14).
Site of a chaotic reunion: Romina turned up unconscious near a fruit cart, a vendor tried to scam Cael, and the party hid below deck through a guard search of the ship before it sailed out past the harbor's closing chains (session 2026-07-28).

### The Arch
An ancient portal structure. The King has secretly ordered its reconstruction, per a royal decree found among the stolen registry cylinders. Leads to "The Path of the Veil."
Location not yet specified beyond "being reconstructed" - purpose and current whereabouts still an open thread.

### The Path of the Veil (*El Camino del Velo*)
A dangerous plane reached via The Arch. Feared and culturally avoided by the Ilshiar/Ashen people. Nothing else about it is known yet.

### The Aurora
Victor's ship, marked with a red flower insignia. Its cargo/operations are unknown - possibly smuggling given Victor's secretive, monocled demeanor.
Confirmed to smuggle people as well as goods; has a concealed hold reached through a hatch in the mast, and is crewed not by living sailors but by a handful of translucent ghost sailors Victor summons by flute. Now sailing toward Ilvana with the full party aboard (session 2026-07-28).

### Ilvana
An inland city, reachable from the coast by a roughly fifteen-day voyage. New destination the party redirected Victor toward, since the Arch proved too far inland to reach without cutting back through the closed city (session 2026-07-28).

### Siltara
A place devastated by the Aeonic being Nexus Vorath in a past incarnation of the party's lives, per a shared dream revelation. Sat directly above the Dunshal city of Belsharun. Nothing else about it is known yet (session 2026-07-28).

### Belsharun
A Dunshal city, apparently the party's origin point in a past incarnation, destroyed or badly damaged by Nexus Vorath. Site of a ritual in which some of the party were captured by an Aeonic, leading to Kalen's pact never to return. Known only through a shared dream revelation, not yet visited in the present timeline (session 2026-07-28).
