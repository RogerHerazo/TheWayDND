# Characters

## Player Characters

### Kalen
Warlock. Eldritch Blast / Repelling Blast, flight speed.
Caused the Registry Office explosion that kicks off the campaign, disguised as an old woman before his cover ran out.
Went into hiding with Erios; helped fake Erios's execution as a rescue cover.
In the plaza brawl, blasted Mole repeatedly and caught Erios's falling sister out of the air.
Recalled that the seaside cave hideout also leads to a stretch of beach outside the city walls, though he'd never actually followed it out (session 2026-07-14). Had Fabio deliver money to Erios's family instead of doing it himself, to avoid his own face being recognized.

### Erios
Ashen (*cenizo*) youth, sorcerer. Scorching Ray, Sorcery Points, can alter his own facial features with magic.
His family (mother, sister, father) was taken hostage by Mole to flush him out.
Took 35 fall damage in Mole's gravity vortex, dropped to 1 HP, then unleashed two rounds of empowered Scorching Ray that shattered Mole's face.
**Formally exiled by his own people** (session 2026-07-14) after Naut's speech on destiny versus will pushed him to throw a punch (which landed on Naut). His father was separately taken hostage by the Crown to flush him out; with nowhere left to go among the Ilshiar, he has no real choice but to travel on with the party.

### Kenshin
Monk. Quarterstaff/longsword, Stunning Strike, Shifu technique.
Cleaved a guard in half and drew first blood on Mole in the first plaza clash.
Knocked unconscious by the gravity vortex fall (40 damage, failed save even with Heroic Inspiration); rescued mid-air by Fabio's mount Chimuelo.
Currently holds a pendant belonging to Naut, which pulsed with unexplained intensity during Naut's speech to Erios (session 2026-07-14). Got tangled in a contradictory cover story with Cael at an Ilshiar checkpoint, resulting in an armed "escort" to the docks; his pet rat Mickey went missing in the confusion.

### Fabio
Heavy armor, giant shield, casts Magic Missile - also summons a spectral flying mount, "Chimuelo."
Struggled to scale rooftops early on due to his gear.
Landed two Magic Missile hits on Mole, including the near-finishing blow. Chimuelo was killed (an arrow through the neck) while rescuing Kenshin.
Tasked with leaving a store credit in Erios's family's name at a local shop, so the money couldn't be easily refused or traced back to Kalen (session 2026-07-14).

### Cael
Monk. Flurry of Blows, Stunning Strike, Slow Fall reaction.
Used Slow Fall to land safely from the gravity vortex.
Delivered the finishing blows on Mole (staff to the temple, then a punch that broke his neck). Carried the wounded Erios during the retreat.
Tried to stop Erios from punching Naut but got his sleeve caught on the table instead (session 2026-07-14). Talked his way past a suspicious checkpoint guard alongside Kenshin, then bribed the waiter Lucas to stage a rock-throwing distraction and shake their guard escort near the docks.

### Romina
Scourge Speech (amplified, unsettling voice), Misty Step, bird familiar/summon, Cause Fear.
Used Misty Step to safely land from the gravity vortex.
Separated from the group during the retreat - fled down a different route alone after her bird failed to steal Mole's staff.
Survived a multi-hour solo escape through the city's sewers (session 2026-07-14): scared off a guard with *Cause Fear*, used *Infestation* on a rat as a deterrent, bribed an Ashen family for safe passage through their home, and crawled for hours through the tunnels before surfacing near a commercial district. Took shelter at Bern's tavern under a mud-and-tears cover story, and was handed a sealed message the next morning directing her to the docks and the ship *The Aurora*.

## NPCs

### Mole
Massive elite royal guard. Gravity-manipulation magic channeled through a heavy, glowing purple staff; moves lightly despite his armor's weight, implying he depends on the staff or heavy concentration.
Tracked Kalen's magical trail from the Registry explosion, took Erios's family hostage, and unleashed a gravity vortex that killed dozens of Ashen civilians and Daef.
**Killed** at the end of the Ilshiar plaza brawl (session 2026-06-30). Notably, his mass and height visibly decreased once he released his staff - the staff itself may be magically tied to his size/power.

### Daef Arlos
Erios's friend. Warned the group that Mole had taken Erios's family hostage.
**Died** in Mole's gravity vortex, along with dozens of Ashen civilians.
His final act mattered beyond his death: the registry cylinder he'd apparently recovered from Erios was found on his body by Naut, and its land-cession document was used to legally cede the entire Ilshiar district to "the Casino" (session 2026-07-14).

### Erios's family (mother, sister, father)
Held hostage by Mole in the plaza via gravity magic pinning them to the ground.
Remained pinned (not swept into the vortex); the sister was briefly lifted 30 feet by Mole before Kalen forced him to drop her, and Kalen cushioned her fall.
As of session 2026-07-14, Erios's father has been publicly arrested and taken to the castle as a hostage to force the "terrorist" out; the party left a store credit in the family's name at a local shop rather than risk handing them cash directly. Mother and sister's status otherwise unchanged.

### Naut
Ilshiar-devoted contact; almost certainly the mysterious figure who sheltered the party at the end of the previous session, though this was never stated outright.
Delivered a long speech to Erios on destiny versus will (session 2026-07-14), praising him for fighting Mole out of pure choice rather than fate - a speech that ended with Erios punching him. Recovered the land-cession cylinder from Daef's body and used it to cede the entire Ilshiar district to "the Casino."
Owns a pendant currently held by Kenshin that pulsed with unexplained intensity during his speech. Referenced a personal quest of his own - finding a sister, and pursuing "a fragment of destiny" - that the party had once asked him to join and that he'd once refused.

### Sergeant Holt
Guard officer overseeing the citywide crackdown following Mole's death; ordered all land routes out of the city sealed, forcing the party toward a sea escape (session 2026-07-14).

### Bern
Kindly tavern keeper in a commercial district outside Ilshiar territory. Took in Romina after finding her mud-covered and crying behind his tavern, giving her a room, food, and a bath without asking for payment - she reminds him of his own estranged daughter, away studying and rarely home.

### Tori
Bern's wife, who runs the tavern's front of house. Openly contemptuous of the Ashen (*cenizos*), whom she blames for the Registry explosion and unrest, and looking forward to the business a public execution would bring. Unknowingly passed Romina a sealed message directing her to the docks and *The Aurora*.

### Lucas
Waiter at *The Witch's Arm* (*El Brazo de Bruja*), a restaurant near the docks. Bribed by Cael and Kenshin with gold smuggled to him inside a beer mug to arrange a rock-throwing distraction against their guard escort.

### Luis
Spice market vendor. Openly human-supremacist, blames the Ashen (*cenizos*) for the Registry explosion.

### Victor
Spice market vendor, distinguished by a red flower insignia tied to his ship, *The Aurora*. Wears a monocle.
Told the party he saw two youths (Kalen and Erios) escaping into a nearby alley; traded saffron with a party member.
Mysterious - his real business and the nature of *The Aurora*'s operations are still unknown.

### The mysterious rescuer
Unidentified figure who opened a door and urged the fleeing party inside during the guard chase, providing last-second refuge.
Identity and motives unknown - possibly connected to an organized Resistance.
