# Characters

## Player Characters

### Kalen
Warlock. Eldritch Blast / Repelling Blast, flight speed.
Caused the Registry Office explosion that kicks off the campaign, disguised as an old woman before his cover ran out.
Went into hiding with Erios; helped fake Erios's execution as a rescue cover.
In the plaza brawl, blasted Mole repeatedly and caught Erios's falling sister out of the air.

### Erios
Ashen (*cenizo*) youth, sorcerer. Scorching Ray, Sorcery Points, can alter his own facial features with magic.
His family (mother, sister, father) was taken hostage by Mole to flush him out.
Took 35 fall damage in Mole's gravity vortex, dropped to 1 HP, then unleashed two rounds of empowered Scorching Ray that shattered Mole's face.

### Kenshin
Monk. Quarterstaff/longsword, Stunning Strike, Shifu technique.
Cleaved a guard in half and drew first blood on Mole in the first plaza clash.
Knocked unconscious by the gravity vortex fall (40 damage, failed save even with Heroic Inspiration); rescued mid-air by Fabio's mount Chimuelo.

### Fabio
Heavy armor, giant shield, casts Magic Missile - also summons a spectral flying mount, "Chimuelo."
Struggled to scale rooftops early on due to his gear.
Landed two Magic Missile hits on Mole, including the near-finishing blow. Chimuelo was killed (an arrow through the neck) while rescuing Kenshin.

### Cael
Monk. Flurry of Blows, Stunning Strike, Slow Fall reaction.
Used Slow Fall to land safely from the gravity vortex.
Delivered the finishing blows on Mole (staff to the temple, then a punch that broke his neck). Carried the wounded Erios during the retreat.

### Romina
Scourge Speech (amplified, unsettling voice), Misty Step, bird familiar/summon, Cause Fear.
Used Misty Step to safely land from the gravity vortex.
Separated from the group during the retreat - fled down a different route alone after her bird failed to steal Mole's staff. Status/whereabouts unknown as of the latest session.

## NPCs

### Mole
Massive elite royal guard. Gravity-manipulation magic channeled through a heavy, glowing purple staff; moves lightly despite his armor's weight, implying he depends on the staff or heavy concentration.
Tracked Kalen's magical trail from the Registry explosion, took Erios's family hostage, and unleashed a gravity vortex that killed dozens of Ashen civilians and Daef.
**Killed** at the end of the Ilshiar plaza brawl (session 2026-06-30). Notably, his mass and height visibly decreased once he released his staff - the staff itself may be magically tied to his size/power.

### Daef Arlos
Erios's friend. Warned the group that Mole had taken Erios's family hostage.
**Died** in Mole's gravity vortex, along with dozens of Ashen civilians.

### Erios's family (mother, sister, father)
Held hostage by Mole in the plaza via gravity magic pinning them to the ground.
Remained pinned (not swept into the vortex); the sister was briefly lifted 30 feet by Mole before Kalen forced him to drop her, and Kalen cushioned her fall. Currently safe as of the latest session, but their situation after the guard's death is unconfirmed.

### Luis
Spice market vendor. Openly human-supremacist, blames the Ashen (*cenizos*) for the Registry explosion.

### Victor
Spice market vendor, distinguished by a red flower insignia tied to his ship, *The Aurora*. Wears a monocle.
Told the party he saw two youths (Kalen and Erios) escaping into a nearby alley; traded saffron with a party member.
Mysterious - his real business and the nature of *The Aurora*'s operations are still unknown.

### The mysterious rescuer
Unidentified figure who opened a door and urged the fleeing party inside during the guard chase, providing last-second refuge.
Identity and motives unknown - possibly connected to an organized Resistance.
