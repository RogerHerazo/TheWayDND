# Factions

### The Crown / Royal Guard
The ruling authority. Secretly ordered the reconstruction of The Arch (see [locations.md](locations.md)) via royal decree.
Deploys elite guards (e.g. Mole) with exceptional magic, plus regular guard patrols. After Mole's death, sent four additional patrols (12 soldiers) with anti-magic flares to hunt the party.
Under Sergeant Holt, launched a citywide house-to-house search for the "terrorists," sealed (or is sealing) all land routes out of the city, and took Erios's father hostage in the castle (session 2026-07-14).

### The Ashen (*cenizos*)
An ethnic/cultural group, targeted by rising human-supremacist sentiment after the Registry explosion (which was blamed on them). Erios and his family are Ashen. Culturally avoid "The Path of the Veil."
Dozens of Ashen civilians were killed by Mole's gravity vortex in the plaza - likely to raise community tension further.
Formally exiled Erios from the community for his violence against Mole (session 2026-07-14), even as Naut - one of their own - held him up as an example of will overcoming destiny.

### Human supremacists
Informal but visible sentiment among parts of the human population, exemplified by the vendor Luis, who openly blames the *cenizos* for the explosion, and by Tori at Bern's tavern.

### The Resistance (unconfirmed)
Possible organized group opposing the Crown. Suggested only by the mysterious figure who gave the party refuge at the end of the 2026-06-30 session - almost certainly Naut (see [characters.md](characters.md)), though his ties to any formal "Resistance" remain unconfirmed.

### The Casino
An organization Kalen (and reportedly others in the party) are affiliated with - described as "adictos a las apuestas" (gambling addicts) but otherwise good people. As of session 2026-07-14, legally owns the entire Ilshiar district, after Naut used a land-cession cylinder recovered from Daef's body to sign it over. Its full nature, membership, and long-term intentions for the district are still unclear.
Naut, revealed to be a fragment of pure Will (see characters.md), mentioned having grown rich at a casino before joining the party's voyage - possibly this same organization, though not confirmed (session 2026-08-11).

### The Dunshal
A people, apparently the party's own origin in a past incarnation, tied to the city of Belsharun. Widely distrusted by the Ilshiar, who see them as dangerous and say they "move in shadows." Use a currency/gemstone called the *ectarion*.
Revealed via a shared party dream and via Victor identifying gemstones the party used to pay their passage; Cael's discovery that he casts no shadow may connect him to this group (session 2026-07-28).

### The Aeonics
Beings tied to the party's original (human) world, seemingly capable of granting a "spark" of power to certain individuals - Kalen and Erios are both called "Aeonic sparks." Nexus Vorath, a hostile Aeonic, was brought into this world by humanity and devastated Siltara and Belsharun in a past incarnation of the party's lives; another Aeonic struck Kalen's pact never to return to Belsharun. Full nature and numbers unknown (session 2026-07-28).
Per Naut - revealed during the voyage to be a fragment of pure Will himself - Aeonics are the living scars left when the primordial forces of Will and Destiny collided - a clash violent enough to form entire planes and worlds, Valkara among them. Each Aeonic is a unique, fragile balance of both forces and is bound permanently to the plane or world it was born in unless that plane is destroyed, at which point some invade other worlds (session 2026-08-11).

### The Silrin (unconfirmed spelling)
A people or race the party's past incarnation apparently lived alongside, seen in a shared dream only as two tree-like figures. Almost nothing else about them is known - not even whether "Silrin" is the correct name (session 2026-07-28).

### The Bielar
A sea-folk at peace with the Ilshiar, though the two peoples rarely make direct contact - they simply know of each other and maintain good relations regardless. Ambushed *The Aurora* to destroy its magical lanterns, whose light was drawing the predator Borges (see characters.md) toward the ship.
After a tense standoff, struck a bargain with the party (brokered by Romina) and granted the ship a magical current to speed it toward Doria, before returning to the sea and making clear they are not allies, merely a people protecting their own territory (session 2026-08-11).

### The Weavers
A fused religion-and-government ruling the human Kingdom of Doria, where praying and paying taxes are treated as one and the same. Judge outsiders through agents called Readers, who decide by sight alone whether a stranger fits into what they call "the Weave" (*la trama*), rather than by papers.
Known (per Victor) to be intolerant of those who don't fit; the Incompletes are the only reliable way for smugglers and travelers to pass through Doria without their notice (session 2026-08-11).
Their culture uses a two-fingered "pulling" gesture and the phrase "que encuentres tu función" ("may you find your function") as both greeting and farewell, performed differently depending on formality; territory extends physically into Ilvana, where Readers in blue conical hats guard the city's gated arches and interrogate every entrant with a ritual "what do you come for," while higher-ranked agents called tejedores (weavers) handle anyone whose "thread" doesn't fit the plan. Ruled, per Ilvana's citizens, by an unseen "Tejedora" (see characters.md) (session 2026-08-18).
Blue-robed Readers and green-robed *tejedores* are confirmed as distinct ranks - Readers flag a mismatched thread, *tejedores* actually re-weave it - and mismatched threads are bound in living gold thread and taken to a vast underground hall beneath Ilvana's tower holding thousands of identical bound captives. A faded, unfamiliar third statue found near a trapped puzzle room hints at a further rank, or a fallen one, the party hasn't identified yet (session 2026-08-25).

### The Incompletes
Heretics within the Kingdom of Doria who reject the Weavers' doctrine. Per Victor, the only reason any smuggled goods or people make it past Doria into Ilvana at all. Not yet contacted by the party (session 2026-08-11).
