# Factions

### The Crown / Royal Guard
The ruling authority. Secretly ordered the reconstruction of The Arch (see [locations.md](locations.md)) via royal decree.
Deploys elite guards (e.g. Mole) with exceptional magic, plus regular guard patrols. After Mole's death, sent four additional patrols (12 soldiers) with anti-magic flares to hunt the party.

### The Ashen (*cenizos*)
An ethnic/cultural group, targeted by rising human-supremacist sentiment after the Registry explosion (which was blamed on them). Erios and his family are Ashen. Culturally avoid "The Path of the Veil."
Dozens of Ashen civilians were killed by Mole's gravity vortex in the plaza - likely to raise community tension further.

### Human supremacists
Informal but visible sentiment among parts of the human population, exemplified by the vendor Luis, who openly blames the *cenizos* for the explosion.

### The Resistance (unconfirmed)
Possible organized group opposing the Crown. Suggested only by the mysterious figure who gave the party refuge at the end of the latest session - not yet confirmed to exist as a formal faction.
