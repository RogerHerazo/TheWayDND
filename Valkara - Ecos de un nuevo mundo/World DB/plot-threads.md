# Plot Threads & Open Questions

## Active

- **The Arch's Purpose** (raised 2026-06-16) - Why is the King ordering the reconstruction of The Arch to "The Path of the Veil," and what do the royal magi hope to achieve by activating it? Still open; as of 2026-07-28, the Arch proved too far inland to reach without cutting back through the closed city, so the party redirected toward Ilvana instead.
- **The Aurora's Secrets** (raised 2026-06-16) - Who is Victor really, and what operations or goods run out of his ship, *The Aurora*? Partially addressed (2026-07-28): the ship smuggles people as well as goods, has a concealed hold, and is crewed by summoned ghost sailors rather than living hands - but Victor's larger business and true nature remain unclear.
- **The Consequences of the Massacre** (raised 2026-06-30) - Mole's gravity vortex killed dozens of Ashen civilians and Daef, and a King's elite guard is now dead. How does the Ilshiar community - and the Crown - react? Partially in motion: the Crown has responded with a citywide manhunt, house searches, and sealed exits, and Erios has been exiled by his own people - but the community's broader reaction is still unfolding.
- **The Gravity Staff** (raised 2026-06-30) - Mole's staff was left in the plaza, and it visibly shrank him once released. Will the royal guard recover it, and is it tied to the royal plot around The Arch?
- **Naut's true agenda** (raised 2026-07-14) - Naut holds a pendant with an unexplained magical link to Kenshin, once refused to help the party find a woman he referred to as his sister, and now speaks of chasing "a fragment of destiny." What does he actually want, and how far does his devotion to the Ilshiar people go? Partially addressed (2026-07-28): a shared party dream suggests he is almost certainly one of two children of a mysterious old man/dragon figure, needed alongside his sister to reseal the Aeonic threat Nexus Vorath.
- **What is "the Casino"?** (raised 2026-07-14) - Naut used Daef's recovered document to legally cede the entire Ilshiar district to this organization Kalen is tied to. Who runs it, what do they want with the district, and how long before the wronged noble notices?
- **Will Erios's father survive the castle?** (raised 2026-07-14) - He was taken hostage specifically to flush out the "terrorist," and Erios is now fleeing the city entirely.
- **Does the family credit reach safely?** (raised 2026-07-14) - The party left a store credit in Erios's family's name rather than risk cash directly. Will fear of the guards keep the shopkeeper from honoring it?
- **Who are the old man's two children, and what is Nexus Vorath?** (raised 2026-07-28) - A shared dream revealed an Aeonic being called Nexus Vorath threatens the world, and that only the old/dragon-man's two children (almost certainly including Naut) can grant the power to defeat, capture, or reseal it. What is Nexus Vorath capable of, who is the second child, and how does the party actually find them?
- **What did Kalen's pact really cost?** (raised 2026-07-28) - The dream revealed Kalen, an "Aeonic spark," struck a bargain with a hostile Aeonic during a ritual in Belsharun to never return there, in exchange for the group's freedom. What are the consequences if that promise is ever broken?
- **Will Romina wake up?** (raised 2026-07-28) - She was found unconscious near the docks with no visible injury and no explanation for what happened between Bern's tavern and the docks. What happened to her, and is she in danger?
- **How does the party survive Ilvana broke?** (raised 2026-07-28) - After paying Victor's steep toll in coin, platinum, and gems, the party is down to almost nothing for a fifteen-day voyage and whatever waits at Ilvana. Where will their next funds or lead come from?

## Resolved

- **Will the party reach the sea in time?** (raised 2026-07-14, resolved 2026-07-28) - With the docks and land routes closing under Sergeant Holt, will Romina, Naut, and the tunnel group (Kalen, Fabio, Erios) all converge with Kenshin and Cael at *The Aurora* before the exits seal? **Resolved:** Despite an unconscious Romina, a guard search of the ship, and a chase flare, the whole group converged and boarded *The Aurora*, which sailed out past the harbor's closing chains toward Ilvana.
- **The River Escape** (raised 2026-06-16, resolved 2026-06-30) - Will Kalen and Erios successfully evade further magical tracking now that they are washing downstream, or will Mole manage to re-acquire their scent? **Resolved:** Kalen and Erios's river dive didn't lead to a separate evasion arc; the 2026-06-30 session picks up with them still in the plaza fight, so no meaningful separation from Mole's tracking occurred.
- **Surviving the Plaza Brawl** (raised 2026-06-16, resolved 2026-06-30) - With one guard pulverized, another sliced in half, and a blue flare disrupting magic, how would the remaining party members finish off the incredibly resilient gravity-wielding elite guard? **Resolved:** The party killed Mole: Kalen's Eldritch Blasts, Erios's empowered Scorching Rays, Fabio's Magic Missile, and Cael's staff-and-punch finisher brought him down, though at heavy cost (Daef and dozens of civilians dead, Kenshin and Erios nearly killed).
- **Is this the Resistance?** (raised 2026-06-30, resolved 2026-07-14) - Who opened the door to shelter the party at the last second, and what did they really want with the group? **Resolved:** The sheltering figure was almost certainly Naut, an Ilshiar-devoted contact who revealed himself the next morning - though whether he's tied to any formal "Resistance" organization is still unconfirmed (see Naut's true agenda, above).
- **Romina's Fate** (raised 2026-06-30, resolved 2026-07-14) - Romina fled the guard patrols alone via a separate route - did she evade capture? **Resolved:** She evaded capture, surviving a multi-hour escape through the city's sewers before taking shelter at Bern's tavern; she's since been directed toward the docks and *The Aurora*.
