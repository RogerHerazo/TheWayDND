# Timeline

### Session - 2026-06-16: The Sparks of Rebellion
Aftermath of Kalen's Registry Office explosion. Anti-Ashen sentiment spikes in the city. The party tracks the elite guard Mole across the rooftops and through the spice market (meeting Luis and Victor).
Kalen and Erios hide in a seaside cave, discover the King's decree to rebuild The Arch, and learn from Daef that Mole has taken Erios's family hostage.
They stage a fake execution as a rescue cover; the confrontation in the Ilshiar plaza begins, ending mid-combat with Kalen and Erios diving into the river.

### Session - 2026-06-30: The Gravity Inversion and the Desperate Flight
Combat in the Ilshiar plaza continues immediately (the river escape did not separate Kalen/Erios from the fight for long). Mole unleashes a gravity vortex that hurls most of the party and nearby civilians 100 feet up; the fall kills Daef and dozens of Ashen civilians and nearly kills Erios and Kenshin.
The party turns the tide - Kalen, Erios, Fabio, and Cael bring Mole down for good, noting his body shrank once he released his staff.
Guard reinforcements and anti-magic flares force a chaotic retreat; Romina gets separated from the group, and the rest find last-second refuge through a door opened by a mysterious stranger.

### Session - 2026-07-14: The Weight of Will and the Long Road to the Sea
The mysterious contact sheltering the party reveals himself as Naut, an Ilshiar-devoted figure who confronts Erios the next morning with a speech about destiny versus will - a confrontation that ends with Erios throwing a punch and learning that Daef's recovered land-cession cylinder was just used to legally sign the entire Ilshiar district over to "the Casino." Formally exiled by his own people, Erios has nowhere left to go but with the party.
Separated during the previous session's retreat, Romina survives a multi-hour crawl through the city's sewers and takes shelter at a tavern run by Bern and his wife Tori, who unknowingly passes her a sealed message directing her to the docks and the ship *The Aurora*.
With Sergeant Holt sealing land routes out of the city and Erios's father taken hostage, the party splits three ways to reach the ship by sea; Kenshin and Cael talk and bribe their way out of an unwanted guard escort just short of the docks.

### Session - 2026-07-28: Passage on the Aurora and the Shadow of Nexus Vorath
Kalen, Fabio, and Erios collapse their escape tunnel on the pursuing guards with a gunpowder trap, reaching a beach outside the walls, while Cael and Kenshin haggle Victor into smuggling the party out aboard *The Aurora*. A chaotic dock scene (an unconscious Romina delivered by Naut, a guard search of the hold) nearly derails the escape before the ship slips the harbor, with Kalen flying Fabio and Erios in on *Chimuelo* to catch it.
The party pays Victor's steep toll in coin, platinum, and Dunshal *ectarion* gems, redirects him toward the inland port of Ilvana since the Arch is unreachable, and discovers Cael casts no shadow.
That night the whole party is pulled into a shared dream in which a shapeshifting old man reveals the true threat: an Aeonic being called Nexus Vorath, which devastated the Dunshal city of Belsharun in a past incarnation of the party's lives, can only be resealed with the help of his two children - one almost certainly Naut.
