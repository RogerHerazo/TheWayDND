# Timeline

### Session - 2026-06-16: The Sparks of Rebellion
Aftermath of [[characters#Kalen|Kalen]]'s Registry Office explosion. Anti-Ashen sentiment spikes in the city. The party tracks the elite guard [[characters#Mole|Mole]] across the rooftops and through [[locations#The spice market|the spice market]] (meeting [[characters#Luis|Luis]] and [[characters#Victor|Victor]]).
Kalen and [[characters#Erios|Erios]] hide in a seaside cave, discover the King's decree to rebuild [[locations#The Arch|The Arch]], and learn from Daef that Mole has taken Erios's family hostage.
They stage a fake execution as a rescue cover; the confrontation in [[locations#The Ilshiar plaza|the Ilshiar plaza]] begins, ending mid-combat with Kalen and Erios diving into the river.

### Session - 2026-06-30: The Gravity Inversion and the Desperate Flight
Combat in [[locations#The Ilshiar plaza|the Ilshiar plaza]] continues immediately (the river escape did not separate [[characters#Kalen|Kalen]]/[[characters#Erios|Erios]] from the fight for long). [[characters#Mole|Mole]] unleashes a gravity vortex that hurls most of the party and nearby civilians 100 feet up; the fall kills Daef and dozens of Ashen civilians and nearly kills Erios and [[characters#Kenshin|Kenshin]].
The party turns the tide - Kalen, Erios, [[characters#Fabio|Fabio]], and [[characters#Cael|Cael]] bring Mole down for good, noting his body shrank once he released his staff.
Guard reinforcements and anti-magic flares force a chaotic retreat; [[characters#Romina|Romina]] gets separated from the group, and the rest find last-second refuge through a door opened by a mysterious stranger.

### Session - 2026-07-14: The Weight of Will and the Long Road to the Sea
The mysterious contact sheltering the party reveals himself as [[characters#Naut|Naut]], an Ilshiar-devoted figure who confronts [[characters#Erios|Erios]] the next morning with a speech about destiny versus will - a confrontation that ends with Erios throwing a punch and learning that Daef's recovered land-cession cylinder was just used to legally sign the entire [[locations#Ilshiar district|Ilshiar district]] over to "[[factions#The Casino|the Casino]]." Formally exiled by his own people, Erios has nowhere left to go but with the party.
Separated during the previous session's retreat, [[characters#Romina|Romina]] survives a multi-hour crawl through the city's sewers and takes shelter at a tavern run by [[characters#Bern|Bern]] and his wife [[characters#Tori|Tori]], who unknowingly passes her a sealed message directing her to [[locations#The docks|the docks]] and the ship *[[locations#The Aurora|The Aurora]]*.
With [[characters#Sergeant Holt|Sergeant Holt]] sealing land routes out of the city and Erios's father taken hostage, the party splits three ways to reach the ship by sea; [[characters#Kenshin|Kenshin]] and [[characters#Cael|Cael]] talk and bribe their way out of an unwanted guard escort just short of the docks.

### Session - 2026-07-28: Passage on the Aurora and the Shadow of Nexus Vorath
[[characters#Kalen|Kalen]], [[characters#Fabio|Fabio]], and [[characters#Erios|Erios]] collapse their escape tunnel on the pursuing guards with a gunpowder trap, reaching a beach outside the walls, while [[characters#Cael|Cael]] and [[characters#Kenshin|Kenshin]] haggle [[characters#Victor|Victor]] into smuggling the party out aboard *[[locations#The Aurora|The Aurora]]*. A chaotic dock scene (an unconscious [[characters#Romina|Romina]] delivered by [[characters#Naut|Naut]], a guard search of the hold) nearly derails the escape before the ship slips the harbor, with Kalen flying Fabio and Erios in on *Chimuelo* to catch it.
The party pays Victor's steep toll in coin, platinum, and Dunshal *ectarion* gems, redirects him toward the inland port of [[locations#Ilvana|Ilvana]] since [[locations#The Arch|the Arch]] is unreachable, and discovers Cael casts no shadow.
That night the whole party is pulled into a shared dream in which a shapeshifting old man reveals the true threat: an [[factions#The Aeonics|Aeonic]] being called [[characters#Nexus Vorath|Nexus Vorath]], which devastated [[factions#The Dunshal|the Dunshal]] city of [[locations#Belsharun|Belsharun]] in a past incarnation of the party's lives, can only be resealed with the help of his two children - one almost certainly Naut.

### Session - 2026-08-11: The Fragment of Will and the Bay of Loose Knots
A stranger riding with the party reveals to [[characters#Kalen|Kalen]] that his current self may be only a ring-sustained projection, his true self trapped in the [[factions#The Aeonics|Aeonic]] dimension since the [[locations#Belsharun|Belsharun]] pact, and explains Valkara's cosmology as born from an ancient collision between Will and Destiny - he himself claims to be a fragment of pure Will searching for his own "wretched sister," the fragment of Destiny, and names "the old man" as his father.
*[[locations#The Aurora|The Aurora]]* is ambushed by [[factions#The Bielar|the Bielar]], a sea-folk at peace with the Ilshiar, who demand the party's magical lanterns be extinguished before a scent-hunting predator called [[characters#Borges|Borges]] finds them; a standoff involving hostages and a stabbed [[characters#Cael|Cael]] is defused when [[characters#Romina|Romina]] strikes an unconventional bargain that finally dissolves a long-standing inner void in her.
Ten days of downtime follow - Cael chronicles events, Kalen learns Counterspell, [[characters#Erios|Erios]] and [[characters#Kenshin|Kenshin]] work through trauma and temper - before [[characters#Victor|Victor]] delivers the party to [[locations#The Kingdom of Doria|the Kingdom of Doria]], a closed human realm ruled by [[factions#The Weavers|the Weavers]], warns them about [[locations#Ilvana|Ilvana]]'s unsettling perfection, and departs for good.

### Session - 2026-08-18: The Threads of Ilvana
Dropped ashore by [[characters#Victor|Victor]] for good, the party treks overland toward [[locations#The Kingdom of Doria|Doria]] and [[locations#Ilvana|Ilvana]] behind a pendant tied to [[characters#Naut|Naut]]'s missing sister, through an unnervingly perfect wilderness of waymarker stones and flawless nature. Naut, confirmed as a fragment of pure Will, weakens visibly as they near Ilvana and retreats into the pendant for safekeeping; after a drunken night and a cryptic warning from a stranger about not fitting Ilvana's "Weave," the party bluffs its way through the Readers' gate into the city.
Once inside, [[characters#Kenshin|Kenshin]] is pulled away by Readers toward the Weaver herself, while [[characters#Romina|Romina]], striking out alone, is captured and bound by living thread and told her existence was never part of the plan.

### Session - 2026-08-25: The Fair Seam and the Hall of a Thousand Threads
While waiting on word of [[characters#Kenshin|Kenshin]], the rest of the party trades a day's labor at an inn, *[[locations#Posada la Costura Justa ("The Fair Seam")|Posada la Costura Justa]]*, for room and board, learning the city's ritual customs and its hierarchy of blue-robed Readers and green-robed *[[factions#The Weavers|tejedores]]*. One by one, [[characters#Fabio|Fabio]], [[characters#Mickey|Mickey]], [[characters#Kalen|Kalen]], and [[characters#Cael|Cael]] are intercepted while running an errand and bound in living gold thread in identical underground chambers, each having their "thread" read into the city's *trama* - Kalen is told his true role is to destabilize [[locations#Ilvana|Ilvana]] entirely.
Only Kenshin breaks free, aided by some hidden strength from the dormant [[characters#Naut|Naut]], waking in a vast hall of thousands of identical bound captives; he frees Mickey, Fabio, Kalen, Cael, [[characters#Erios|Erios]], and [[characters#Romina|Romina]] herself.
Climbing toward the tower's upper floors, the freed party survives a vicious thread-trap that nearly kills Kenshin, solves a color-coded floor puzzle tied to the city's ranks, and bursts in on three *tejedores* mid-meal just as combat begins.

### Session - 2026-09-08: Lunch Interrupted and the Vault of Closed Consultations
The party kills the three *[[factions#The Weavers|tejedores]]* caught at lunch - one disintegrated by [[characters#Kalen|Kalen]], one beheaded by [[characters#Fabio|Fabio]] after a false surrender, and the last self-detonating in a fireball that sets the room ablaze.
Scouting the surrounding rooms, they find a training hall of *tejedores*, an oblivious old janitor, and a catalogued vault of confiscated and unexplained artifacts, which they loot thoroughly despite a trap.
[[characters#Romina|Romina]] binds [[characters#Mickey|Mickey]] to her curse of *Undying* just as trainees discover the scorched dining room; [[characters#Kenshin|Kenshin]] cuts down two of them, but the third escapes behind a locked door that Fabio kicks in as the session ends.

### Session - 2026-09-23: The Needle Poem and the Architect of Destiny
The tower's oblivious janitor, mistaking [[characters#Fabio|Fabio]] for an apprentice, teaches him and [[characters#Kenshin|Kenshin]] a poem learned from the long-missing Architect [[characters#Caelan Fray|Caelan Fray]]; after looting a locked bedroom for red-and-white robes as disguises, the party uses that poem to solve a room of symbol-marked pillars and climbs to the tower's summit.
There they find the girl [[characters#Emma|Emma]] - apparently [[characters#Naut|Naut]]'s sister - kneeling on the symbol of Destiny as the city's threads pour out of her, guarded by the Architect [[characters#Solene Brey|Solene Brey]].
Fabio hurls [[characters#Mickey|Mickey]], wearing Naut's pendant, at Emma, and the fight that follows goes badly: Solene disarms and terrifies half the party, unravels [[characters#Cael|Cael]] into golden thread, and clubs Mickey senseless, though [[characters#Romina|Romina]] severs two of Emma's threads and Kenshin finally draws blood as the session breaks off mid-battle.

### Session - 2026-10-07: The Soul in the Pendant
The fight resumes: [[characters#Romina|Romina]] revives [[characters#Mickey|Mickey]], [[characters#Kalen|Kalen]] batters [[characters#Solene Brey|Solene Brey]] through a sofa, and [[characters#Kenshin|Kenshin]] remembers the *Tijeras* dagger and cuts [[characters#Emma|Emma]]'s threads, putting real fear in Solene's eyes. Mickey bridges the floor with ice and touches Emma with [[characters#Naut|Naut]]'s pendant, which proves to be a fragment of her own soul; she stops weaving and wakes.
Solene answers by pulling in thousands of grey threads from her past 'corrections' and drawing six massive cords from the floor. [[characters#Cael|Cael]] is cut free of his thread prison and restored, but a final flash makes every figure in the room look identical to Kenshin, and Emma ends the session by linking a thread to him: "you will be able to see."
