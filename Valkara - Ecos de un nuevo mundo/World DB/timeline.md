# Timeline

### Session - 2026-06-16: The Sparks of Rebellion
Aftermath of Kalen's Registry Office explosion. Anti-Ashen sentiment spikes in the city. The party tracks the elite guard Mole across the rooftops and through the spice market (meeting Luis and Victor).
Kalen and Erios hide in a seaside cave, discover the King's decree to rebuild The Arch, and learn from Daef that Mole has taken Erios's family hostage.
They stage a fake execution as a rescue cover; the confrontation in the Ilshiar plaza begins, ending mid-combat with Kalen and Erios diving into the river.

### Session - 2026-06-30: The Gravity Inversion and the Desperate Flight
Combat in the Ilshiar plaza continues immediately (the river escape did not separate Kalen/Erios from the fight for long). Mole unleashes a gravity vortex that hurls most of the party and nearby civilians 100 feet up; the fall kills Daef and dozens of Ashen civilians and nearly kills Erios and Kenshin.
The party turns the tide - Kalen, Erios, Fabio, and Cael bring Mole down for good, noting his body shrank once he released his staff.
Guard reinforcements and anti-magic flares force a chaotic retreat; Romina gets separated from the group, and the rest find last-second refuge through a door opened by a mysterious stranger.
