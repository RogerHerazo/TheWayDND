# D&D Session Summary: Lunch Interrupted and the Vault of Closed Consultations

## Summary
Picking up mid-ambush, the party tore through the three *tejedores* they had caught at lunch: Kalen disintegrated one with a barrage of *Eldritch Blast*, Fabio beheaded a second who had feigned surrender only to magically freeze Kenshin in place, and the last, cornered and burning, detonated himself in a fireball that set the whole room alight. With the flames stamped out, the party scouted the surrounding doors - a training hall full of *tejedores* drilling on straw dummies, a sitting room where a tiny old man mopped the floor, oblivious - and slipped into a vast, meticulously catalogued storeroom whose ledger, the *Registro de objetos de consulta cerrada*, listed the tower's confiscated and unexplained magic items. Despite a trap that zapped Fabio for lifting the ledger, the party looted the vault thoroughly, walking away with an endless spool of thread, a dagger engraved *Tijeras*, a ring that resists binding thread, a bottomless pouch of gunpowder, and more. Just as Romina bound Mickey to her own curse of *Undying*, trainees from the hall stumbled onto the scorched dining room and called for the alarm - Kenshin cut two of them in half, but the third fled and locked himself behind a door, and the session ended with Fabio kicking it down.

---

## Key Events

### 1. The Lunch Ambush
* **A blade of fire:** Kenshin opened the fight by drawing his katana in a single motion that sent a sheet of flame through the room, carving a deep, cauterized gash across one *tejedor*'s chest.
* **Kicks that wouldn't land:** Cael vaulted across the tables capoeira-style to kick two of the *tejedores*, but both of his *Stunning Strike* attempts failed to lock up their muscles, and he settled atop a table in a defensive stance.
* **Armor of ice:** Romina wrapped herself and Cael in *Armor of Agathys*, while Fabio planted himself in front of Mickey to shield him from any attack.
* **Erased from existence:** Kalen, fueled by pure hatred after being told his thread was meant to unmake Ilvana, poured *Eldritch Blast* into one *tejedor* until only his legs hit the floor - brushing off Fabio's complaint about ruined loot with "I hate these people, I want to erase them from existence."
* **The weavers strike back:** One *tejedor* called down twin columns of fire on Kalen, who sidestepped them entirely, then conjured a dagger of pure energy that stopped just short of his eyes; the other dropped to his knees and surrendered.

### 2. A False Surrender and a Burning Room
* **Threats over mercy:** Romina blasted the surrendering *tejedor* with *Ray of Frost* and, voice amplified by *Scourge Speech*, warned him that surrender would not undo what his people had done to them - though her intimidation left him visibly unmoved.
* **A touch on the foot:** When Kenshin strode past the kneeling man, the *tejedor* brushed his ankle with a prepared spell, and Kenshin, failing a will save, was gripped by an overwhelming urge to retreat, freezing in place with his attack wasted.
* **Cael connects the dots:** Having seen the subtle touch, Cael drove a knee into the man's face and followed with a *Flurry of Blows*, and Fabio finished him with a radiant, flame-wreathed sword strike that sent his head rolling across the table.
* **A final detonation:** The last *tejedor*, robes smoking, drew his hands into his sleeves and exploded in a fireball - Fabio caught the blast behind his shield, Cael rolled under a table just in time, while Kenshin and a dazed Mickey (who had all but forgotten he was in the fight) took it head-on, and the explosion set fire to the tables and incinerated the bodies.
* **Stamping out the fire:** The party smothered the flames with tablecloths and boots; Erios wandered in afterward, having lingered behind staring at the golden-robed woman's mural in the puzzle room, and Mickey thanked Fabio for protecting him before climbing onto his shoulder.

### 3. Behind Every Door
* **The training hall:** Fabio cracked open one door to find *tejedores* drilling against straw-and-cloth dummies, and eased it shut again without a sound, aided by his gloves.
* **The old man with the mop:** Behind another door, a small sitting room with three armchairs and a fireplace held a tiny, elderly man mopping the floor with his back to the party, completely unaware of them.
* **Kenshin's boiling blood:** Approaching a third door, Kenshin felt his blood begin to boil and an urge to charge in and attack welling up from the gem he carries, but managed to master it.
* **The vault:** Kalen opened the last door onto an enormous storeroom of boxes, sacks, and barrels on numbered metal racks, with a small desk holding a ledger titled *Registro de objetos de consulta cerrada*; when Fabio lifted it, a hidden statue clicked to life and fired a ray that struck him for eight damage before its eyes went dark.

### 4. Looting the Tower's Secrets
* **The ledger's entries:** Fabio read out the catalogue, which included a *false-reading mirror* placed by Architect Solene Brey to verify suspiciously clean threads, *gloves of firm thread* confiscated from a Doria smuggler who used them to climb the city walls undetected, a *spool of thread* that never runs out, a jar of *permanent ink* reserved for records of indefinite imprisonment, a nameless *dagger engraved "Tijeras"* deposited by Architect Caelan Fray with no explanation, and an *uninscribed ring* recovered from a spool that stopped yielding thread, granting minor resistance to thread restraint.
* **Taking what they could:** The party claimed the ring, the climbing gloves, the endless spool, the ink, and the *Tijeras* dagger, leaving the large mirror behind.
* **Treasures in the crates:** Romina opened a copper-and-glass rack of five scrolls only to watch four burn when she unsealed the middle one, whose letters spoke to her and vanished as she read, leaving her changed (*Observant*); Kalen found a small black pouch tied with red thread that pours out gunpowder for as long as it is held upside down; Fabio found a gold ring rimmed in green that steadily mends his wounds; Kenshin found a pair of metal bracers; and Cael found a scroll bearing a common-tongue poem wishing him good luck (*Lucky*).
* **A cape and a new bed:** Erios, tapping crates to find one that sounded muffled, uncovered a seamless, ornately inscribed crimson cape; Mickey found a giant crate of straw containing a pair of tiny boots in his size, though he was far more excited about stuffing his hat with straw for a new bed.

### 5. The Alarm
* **A share of the curse:** Romina cast *Ailment Control* on Mickey, who shrugged off the spell the first time.
* **Discovered:** Trainees from the hall opened the door onto the scorched dining room and gasped, "What happened here? Should we sound the alarm?" - prompting Kenshin to burst out to meet them and the others to scramble after him.
* **Undying:** As the fight began, Romina tried again, and this time the spell took hold, sharing her accursed *Undying* with Mickey so that he cannot die until it is lifted by *Dispel Magic*, *Greater Restoration*, or *Remove Curse* - "Mickey, share a little of my pain."
* **Two clean cuts:** Kenshin, using *Shifu* and *Jinpu*, sliced two of the trainees in half with single strokes, the robe falling from one to reveal a very young man.
* **A locked door:** The last trainee fled through a door and locked it behind him; when Fabio kicked it off its hinges, the old man was still mopping inside, oblivious, as Fabio and Kenshin charged through and the session ended.

## Open Questions
* **Who are the Architects?** The ledger names Solene Brey and Caelan Fray as "Architects" with authority to deposit artifacts in the vault - is this the hidden third rank the faded statue hinted at, and how high in Ilvana does it reach?
* **What is the *Tijeras* dagger for?** Caelan Fray left a nameless dagger engraved "Scissors" in a city ruled by thread and refused to record its purpose - was it a weapon meant to cut the *trama* itself, and why did he hide it?
* **Will the alarm sound?** One trainee escaped through a locked door, the dining room is a charred ruin with three dead *tejedores*, and the tower's guards have yet to arrive - how much time does the party have left?
* **What does Undying cost Mickey?** Romina has bound her own curse to Kenshin's rat with a cryptic "share a little of my pain" - what is that pain, and what will Mickey carry with it?
* **What is stirring in Kenshin's gem?** His blood boiled with the urge to attack the moment he neared a room of *tejedores* - is this the same anger he's been fighting since taking up his cursed collar, and could it overwhelm him at the wrong moment?
