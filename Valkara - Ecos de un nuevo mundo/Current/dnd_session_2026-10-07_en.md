# D&D Session Summary: The Soul in the Pendant

## Summary

The session resumed mid-battle in the chamber of Destiny atop Ilvana's tower. With Cael unraveled and Mickey down, the party's priorities were to save Mickey, keep the Architect Solene Brey pinned down, and cut Emma's threads with the *Tijeras* dagger. Romina recovered and revived Mickey, Kalen's Repelling Blasts smashed Solene through a sofa into the wall, and Kenshin sliced Emma's threads cleanly, the first thing all fight that put real terror in Solene's eyes. Mickey then crossed an ice bridge and touched Emma with Naut's pendant, which proved to be a fragment of her own soul; Emma stopped weaving and awoke. Solene answered by drawing on old grey threads from her thousands of 'corrections,' wrapping herself in new power and scolding Emma that she 'only knows how to read.' In the chaos Kenshin cut Cael free of a thread prison, but a final flash left every figure in the room looking identical to Kenshin, and the session ended as the awakened Emma told him 'you will be able to see' and linked a thread to him.

---

## Key Events

### 1. Saving Mickey and Battering Solene
* **Romina's rescue:** Romina used *Misty Step* to reach the coffee table, pulled the motionless Mickey out from under it, and rolled back over a severed thread to safety, clutching him as he failed a death save. She noticed Naut's pendant briefly go dark and cold in his arms.
* **Kalen's furious barrage:** Trading accuracy for damage, Kalen's Repelling Blasts knocked Solene Brey into the sofa, broke its back, and slammed her into the wall with her robe burned. Several later blasts missed and riddled the wall.
* **Erios and Fabio press:** Erios used *Misty Step* to appear in front of the prone Solene and hit her with *Thunder Wave*, though she resisted most of it. Fabio burned a resonance fragment from the forest creatures to cast an acid *Chromatic Orb*, which she calmly dodged, but his follow-up longsword strikes hit.
* **A knot in the air:** When Erios pointed toward where Cael had vanished, Solene tugged at the air and only Erios saw a fingernail-sized golden knot shoot from that spot elsewhere in the room.

### 2. Cael's Prison and the Tijeras Cuts
* **Inside the skein:** Cael found himself alive in a small chamber walled with golden thread over a pure white floor, where heads and bodies of thread-people formed and unraveled around him. He punched through the wall with his staff, causing a tiny flash of light visible in the chamber of Destiny.
* **Kenshin remembers the dagger:** With Heroic Inspiration granted to everyone for the decisive fight, Kenshin recalled the *Tijeras* dagger and sliced two of Emma's threads like butter, with no shockwave or harm to himself.
* **Solene's warning:** Solene protested that those threads were the lives of many in Ilvana and important parts of the *trama*; Kenshin brushed her off and urged the party to cut the rest.
* **The knot glows:** The golden knot of Destiny began to glow, and Romina mastered a brief surge of fear, more for her companions than herself.

### 3. Solene Strikes Back, Mickey Revives
* **Erios flattened:** Solene commanded Erios to 'Fall' and clubbed him with her staff; he answered with *Comic Timing*, hitting her with a pie and cartoonishly flattening and bouncing off the blows while resisting part of the damage.
* **An error of the trama:** Solene called Erios an error that had to be corrected, and he retorted she only said it because he is Ashen. Her command to another party member to 'Flee' failed.
* **Mickey returns:** Romina cast *Cure Wounds* and raised Mickey like Simba; he revived instantly, complained she was squeezing him, and saw that Cael was gone. Romina's attempt to reach the flash point was nearly derailed by a catapulting thread, undone with her Heroic Inspiration.
* **Cracked confidence:** As Kalen's next barrage missed entirely and Solene laughed that the *trama* sustained her, Kenshin twirled the *Tijeras* and saw real terror in her eyes for the first time.

### 4. The Pendant Wakes Emma
* **Ice bridge:** Doubling his speed with his boots, Mickey showed Kenshin the pendant, then raised an *Ice Construct* bridge step by step over the floor threads to the kneeling Emma and touched her with it, begging her to wake up.
* **A fragment of her soul:** The pendant proved to be a fragment of Emma's own soul; on a 30% gamble, Emma stopped weaving, looked at Mickey and the pendant, and her remaining threads flickered and began vanishing. Solene's command made Mickey drop it, and it slid to Emma's feet.
* **Solene's corrections:** Solene's woven robe dissolved into golden strands that sank into the floor. Kneeling, she declared her thousands of 'corrections' had all served this moment as old grey threads full of unfinished voices poured toward her.
* **Solene reborn:** After a blinding flash yanked everyone in different directions, Solene stood wrapped in grey threads, drew six massive cords from the floor, and told Emma to return to work because she 'only knows how to read,' not decide. Several new figures appeared around the room.

### 5. Cael Restored and a Room of Lookalikes
* **Twelve missiles:** Ignoring a motionless newcomer, Erios used a Quickened Spell to fire two 4th-level *Magic Missiles*, twelve darts that all struck Solene and left her visibly battered. Fabio warded himself against attacks and charged his sword with lightning.
* **Cael cut free:** Cael pierced a distinct glowing thread from inside his prison, triggering another reality-shuffling flash. Kenshin cut that thread, and it fell and re-formed into Cael, whole again.
* **Who is who:** Kenshin found every figure in the chamber looking identical, while unfamiliar voices gave him contradictory orders about which was the real Solene. In the confusion someone checked on 'Cael' only to hear 'I'm Fabio!'; only one true Solene was among them.
* **Emma's thread:** The awakened Emma looked at Kenshin, said 'You, you will be able to see,' and sent a thread from her outstretched hand to connect with him as the session ended.

## Open Questions
* **What will Kenshin see?:** Emma connected a thread to him with the promise that he would be able to see. Will it pierce the illusion of identical figures, and what does binding to the fragment of Destiny do to him?
* **Who really decides destiny?:** Solene told Emma she only reads and does not decide, suggesting the Architects, not Emma, steer Ilvana's *trama*. What power do the grey threads of her 'corrections' give her now?
* **What are the lookalikes?:** New figures appeared after the flash, and every person in the room now looks the same to Kenshin, with voices urging him to strike different targets. Which one is Solene, and who are the others?
* **What happens to Ilvana?:** Solene claimed the cut threads were the lives of many citizens, and Emma's remaining threads are fading now that she has stopped weaving. What becomes of a city whose whole weave is unraveling?
* **Will Naut emerge?:** His pendant, a fragment of Emma's soul, now lies at her feet as she wakes. Can Naut reunite with his sister, and is this room of pure Destiny still too much for him?
