# D&D Session Summary: The Fragment of Will and the Bay of Loose Knots

## Summary
Ten days into the voyage toward Ilvana, a stranger riding along with the party revealed a shattering truth to Kalen over drinks: the Kalen who has adventured with the group is only a projection sustained by a ring, while his true self has been trapped in the Aeonic dimension ever since the Belsharun pact - and that same stranger, a self-described fragment of pure Will searching for his own "wretched sister," the fragment of Destiny, laid out the whole cosmology of how Aeonics are born from the ancient collision between Will and Destiny. That conversation was cut short when *The Aurora* jolted to a dead stop, besieged by the Bielar, a sea-folk at peace with the Ilshiar, who boarded to destroy the ship's magical lanterns before a scent-hunting predator called *Borges* found them. A tense standoff - Fabio held at trident-point, Cael stabbed twice after trying to take the Bielar leader hostage - dissolved only when Romina made an unconventional offer that healed something old and unspoken inside her, and the Bielar rewarded the party with a magical current to speed them on their way. Ten quiet days of study, training, and an ominous glimpse of a serpentine shadow in the deep followed, before Victor finally delivered the party to the cliffs of the Kingdom of Doria - a purely human realm ruled by a sect called the Weavers - and dropped them at a remote inlet with a warning about Ilvana's unsettling perfection before sailing off for good.

---

## Key Events

### 1. The Ring, the Patron, and a Fragment of Will
* **A projection wearing a ring:** At the table with the rest of the party nearby, a stranger who had been riding along told Kalen plainly that the Kalen everyone knew was only a projection of his true self, sustained through a ring - and that the moment the ring came off, he would revert to who he really was.
* **The patron's lie:** Kalen protested that he felt real and physical, then recalled that the Aeonic he'd struck his pact with in Belsharun had lied to him: he was told he would die, when in truth he would simply be trapped in that Aeonic's dimension - and, the stranger confirmed, Kalen was apparently the only one of the group never to have gotten free of it.
* **An explosion waiting on a finger:** The stranger warned that the ring currently held a large portion of accumulated Aeonic power, and that removing or breaking it would unleash it as a catastrophic explosion; when Kalen half-jokingly grabbed at the ring to test him, the stranger's only concern was that he personally wouldn't be present to witness the blast, though he made clear he intended to *feel* it regardless - a hunger that left Kalen with no real argument to offer back.
* **The cosmology of Valkara:** Pressed on why Aeonics exist at all, the stranger explained that they are the living scars of the primordial collision between Will and Destiny, a clash violent enough to form entire planes and worlds - Valkara among them - with each surviving Aeonic a unique, fragile balance of both forces, bound permanently to the world it was born from unless that world is destroyed.
* **A fragment of pure Will:** The stranger revealed himself to be exactly such a fragment - the best of the pieces that broke off from Will itself during one such clash - traveling with the party (after a stint growing rich at a casino) to find his own "wretched sister," the counterpart fragment of Destiny; asked who his father was, he answered simply: "the old man," confirming his tie to the figure from the party's shared dream.

### 2. The Bielar Ambush
* **The ship stops dead:** *The Aurora* jolted to a sudden halt at sea, its rigging straining as barrels and dice went flying, though nothing outside the ship seemed to be moving it - Victor, roused from a drunken stupor, confirmed this had never happened before and had no explanation.
* **Lights going out:** Ghostly crew swarmed to investigate as the ship's magical lanterns began winking out one by one, struck from the water by something like thrown spears; Kenshin climbed the mast to scout while Fabio and Kenshin advanced toward the darkened bow with torches, only for their light to be knocked out and a blade pressed to both their throats in the same instant.
* **The Bielar surface:** Fish-folk called the Bielar emerged from the water demanding the party extinguish and destroy the ship's magical lanterns, revealing that the light was drawing a magic-hunting predator, *Borges*, that had been stalking them for some time; Kenshin recognized the Bielar as a people at peace with the Ilshiar, rarely in contact but on good terms.
* **Hostages at blade-point:** As the Bielar warriors held tridents and daggers on Fabio and Kenshin, Cael slipped through the shadows - unnoticed even by his own allies - and pressed a dagger to the throat of the old woman leading the Bielar, demanding a surrender.
* **A counter-strike:** The old woman answered by driving two daggers into Cael, one into his abdomen and one grazing his heart, laughing that he was too slow; only a round of desperate diplomacy - Fabio urging calm, Kenshin lowering his weapons - kept the standoff from turning into a slaughter.

### 3. Romina's Bargain
* **An unconventional offer:** With neither side willing to back down and the Bielar unmoved by gold, Romina stepped in and offered herself and the group's company for the night instead, using her *Cold Aura* and persuasion to seal the deal.
* **A void filled:** The night resolved the standoff, but for Romina it did something more - a long-standing inner emptiness and a nagging sense that she owed some unnamed debt both vanished afterward, replaced by something closer to a feeling of credit owed *to* her.
* **A current instead of a crew:** In exchange for peace, the Bielar leader offered the party a specially channeled ocean current to carry the ship swiftly and silently through the night, since her people knew a shortcut *Borges* couldn't follow; she declined the ship's maps and logs when offered as payment, since they weren't the party's to give.
* **A parting warning:** Before diving back into the sea, the Bielar leader made clear her people weren't allies, only a group keeping a dangerous beast away from their own territory - and that the ghost sailors' lanterns would have to stay dark until Victor could repair and relight them safely in port.

### 4. Ten Days on a Stolen Current
* **A chronicle in the making:** Cael spent the downtime writing everything the party had done into an in-world record, treating it as the group's official, if embarrassingly detailed, account of events.
* **A new spell, a spent gem:** Having watched the mysterious stranger cast *Counterspell* during the earlier confrontation, Kalen spent one of the party's remaining Dunshal *ectarion* gems to learn the spell for himself.
* **Processing exile:** Erios spent most of the voyage trying to work through the trauma of his exile, picking up what little he could about sailing from the ghost crew and sparring and practicing magic with Kalen between quieter moments.
* **A collar's temper:** Kenshin meditated through much of the trip, working to control the surges of anger he'd noticed building in himself since he began wearing his cursed collar.
* **A shadow in the deep:** On the first night of the crossing, the party glimpsed a massive, serpentine shape breach the ocean's surface some distance from the ship before vanishing back beneath the waves - a reminder that whatever *Borges* was, it was still out there.

### 5. Landfall in Doria
* **Cliffs and a closed kingdom:** After exiting the current, *The Aurora* approached a line of towering, gull-quiet cliffs; Victor announced they had reached the Kingdom of Doria, a realm he refused to enter himself and could only drop the party near, at a remote inlet called the Bay of Loose Knots (*Bahía de los Nudos Sueltos*).
* **The Weavers and their Readers:** Victor explained that Doria is ruled by a fused religion-and-government called the Weavers, who judge outsiders not by papers but through agents called Readers - people who simply look at a stranger and decide whether they fit into what the Weavers call "the Weave" (*la trama*).
* **The Incompletes:** To reach Ilvana without running afoul of the Weavers, Victor recommended seeking out the Incompletes, heretics who reject Weaver doctrine and are the only reason any smuggled goods - or people - make it into Ilvana at all.
* **Ilvana's unsettling perfection:** By reputation, Victor said, Ilvana is a place where no one argues, steals, or has to be asked twice - everyone simply performs their role without oversight - a description that unsettled him even as he admitted part of him found it appealing.
* **A confirmation and a farewell:** Victor confirmed Doria is a purely human kingdom with no other Valkarian peoples living there, eyeing Erios and Cael with new interest at the news, before wishing the party well and sailing off for good; the session ended there, with the whole party leveling up.

## Open Questions
* **Is Kalen's true self really trapped?** The stranger's claim - that the party's Kalen is only a ring-sustained projection while his real self remains in the Aeonic dimension since Belsharun - was never verified; is it true, and if so, can he ever be made whole again?
* **What happens if the ring breaks?** The stranger made clear he's anticipating the explosion the ring would unleash, almost hungrily - is he actually looking for a way to trigger it, and what would it mean for Kalen if he succeeded?
* **Is the stranger's sister the same sister Naut is searching for?** Both are described as children of "the old man," one a fragment of Will and the other of Destiny - does that mean Naut and this new stranger are hunting for the very same woman, or two different sisters entirely?
* **Will *Borges* catch up again?** The beast was only avoided, not defeated, and the glimpsed serpentine shadow suggests it's still tracking the party from the deep - what happens when the lanterns have to be relit?
* **Can the party pass a Reading?** With Doria housing no other Valkarian races, and Readers judging outsiders on sight rather than paperwork, will Erios and Cael - or anyone else - be able to slip through without drawing dangerous attention?
