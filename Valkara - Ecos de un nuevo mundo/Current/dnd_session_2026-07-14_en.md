# D&D Session Summary: The Weight of Will and the Long Road to the Sea

## Summary
In the wreckage of the plaza battle, a mysterious Ilshiar contact named Naut finds the party's hideout and offers them a night's shelter, then confronts Erios the next morning with a searing speech about destiny versus will - one that ends in a thrown punch and the revelation that Daef's dying act, a stolen land-cession cylinder, has just been used to legally sign the entire Ilshiar district over to "the Casino." Exiled from his own people for the violence he caused, Erios has little choice but to join the party's road. Meanwhile, separated during the retreat, Romina survives a grueling solo escape through the city's sewers and washes up, filthy and half-drowned, at a tavern whose keeper mistakes her for someone worth protecting. With land routes closing under Sergeant Holt's orders and Erios's father dragged off as a hostage, the group splits three ways to reach the ship *The Aurora* by sea - and Kenshin and Cael talk, bluff, and bribe their way out of an unwanted guard escort just steps from the docks.

---

## Key Events

### 1. Naut's Vigil
* **A stranger at the door:** Naut, an Ilshiar contact previously glimpsed only as the figure who sheltered the party at the end of the last session, revealed himself properly. He explained he came running the moment he saw the aftermath spilling out of the Ilshiar district - not out of concern for the party, but because he cares only about the Ilshiar people themselves.
* **A promise to explain later:** Visibly shaken by whatever he'd learned about what "one of them" had done, Naut declined to say more that night, telling the group to rest while he kept watch. The party settled in for a long rest, undisturbed as guards searched nearby homes but never the one sheltering them.

### 2. Romina's Sewer Escape and the Tavern's Kindness
* **Scaring off the first guard:** Alone and cut off from the group, Romina used *Cause Fear* to send one guard running, but immediately found two more squads - a dozen soldiers - closing in on her from both directions down the alley.
* **A rat, a plague, and a stealthy retreat:** Ducking into a side alley, she cast *Infestation* on a nearby rat to wreathe it in a small cloud of biting insects as a "don't come this way" deterrent, then used Stealth to zigzag deeper into the maze of alleys. The guards' patrol passed by without noticing her.
* **Hiding under a blanket, hiding in a stranger's home:** She spent the better part of an hour holed up under a blanket-covered stack of crates before a mounted commander arrived, ordering his men to search every house and every alley in the district for the "terrorists," with anyone uncooperative to be dragged to the castle for immediate judgment. When a guard finished tossing one house and moved toward hers, Romina slipped inside through a window ahead of him, found an Ashen couple with a newborn, and bought their silence with two gold coins before escaping out through their outhouse hole into the tunnels below.
* **Hours in the sewer:** She crawled for hours through a cramped, filth-choked tunnel too narrow to do anything but drag herself along on her stomach, passing occasional shafts leading up to other outhouses (one dropping something unpleasant directly onto her head) while hearing the muffled sounds of the raid continuing above - crying children, a barking dog, Ashen families being roughed up.
* **Surfacing in a stranger's city:** She eventually reached a wider junction, submerged fully, and climbed a long ladder up into a manhole in what turned out to be a modern commercial district, well outside Ilshiar territory. Filthy beyond words, she snuck into the mud-and-slop pig pen behind a nearby tavern and rolled herself in the muck to sell a cover story.
* **Bern's tavern:** The tavern keeper, Bern, came out at the commotion and found her crying and covered in mud; she claimed she'd been shoved into it by fleeing "cenizos." Something about her reminded him of his own estranged daughter, away studying and rarely home, and he gave her a room, a bath, food, and refused the three gold coins she offered in thanks.
* **Tori and the envelope:** The next morning she met Bern's wife, Tori - openly contemptuous of the Ashen, whom she blamed for the Registry explosion and the citywide unrest, and eager for the business a public execution would bring the tavern. Before Romina left, Tori handed her a small sealed envelope that had been dropped off for her: instructions to head to the docks and find the ship *The Aurora*.

### 3. The Weight of Will
* **A speech about destiny and will:** The next morning, with the party gathered and Erios seated beside Kalen, Naut fixed his stare on Erios and delivered a long, pointed monologue: killing Mole wasn't what impressed him - what impressed him was that an Ilshiar had chosen to fight at all, against everything his people's culture and "destiny" told him to do, for no reason but that he wanted to. Kenshin, who was holding Naut's pendant, felt it begin to pulse harder and harder as the speech went on.
* **A punch and a truth:** When Naut pressed further - naming Daef's death and the deaths of the Ashen villagers in the plaza as consequences of Erios's choice to act - Erios lunged to strike him. Cael tried to intervene but his sleeve snagged on the table, and the punch landed square on Naut, who took it without retaliating and kept talking.
* **Daef's last gift:** Naut then produced the registry cylinder Erios had dropped while fleeing the explosion - recovered, he revealed, from Daef's body. Inside was a land-cession document transferring a noble's holdings. Naut resealed it and used it to legally place the entire Ilshiar district under the ownership of "the Casino," buying the party a window of safety before the wronged noble realizes what happened.
* **Exile, and an uneasy alliance:** Naut confirmed that Erios's spark, however impressive, wasn't enough to spare him from being formally exiled by his own people - leaving him with no real home to return to and no real choice but to travel with the party. He agreed to look after Erios's people himself and pointed to two other trusted allies already among the group, then gestured to Kalen as "another spark" alongside Erios. He also hinted, cryptically, at a personal stake of his own: a sister he was once asked (and once refused) to help find, and a fragment of destiny he now means to chase down himself.

### 4. Charting the Escape by Sea
* **Land routes closing:** With Sergeant Holt expected to seal every landward exit from the city, the group agreed the only way out was by sea - most likely aboard *The Aurora*, the ship belonging to the spice-market vendor Victor.
* **The cave route:** Kalen recalled that the seaside cave hideout led to a stretch of beach outside the city walls, though he'd never actually followed it all the way out. The group split three ways: Cael and Kenshin would go to the docks to arrange passage; Naut would use his information network to track down Romina; Kalen, Fabio, and Erios would take the tunnel to the beach and meet the ship there.
* **A gift for a family under guard:** Before leaving, the group insisted Erios take money for his family, who were now under guard - his father had been publicly arrested and hauled off toward the castle as a hostage to force the "terrorist" out. Since delivering cash directly risked exposing the family (and since Erios's own people would likely refuse or report a direct handout), the group settled on leaving a standing store credit in the family's name at a local shop instead, with Fabio handling the errand to avoid Kalen's face being recognized.

### 5. The Checkpoint Charade
* **Caught in a contradiction:** Slipping out of Ilshiar territory, Kenshin and Cael were stopped at a checkpoint and questioned about their business in the district. Their cover stories clashed - different hotel names, crossed timelines about when they'd arrived and whether they'd eaten breakfast - and the guards, splitting them up to compare answers, caught every inconsistency.
* **An escort "for their safety":** Rather than detaining them, the lead guard decided to assign a heavy guard and two light guards to personally escort the pair all the way to the docks, ostensibly for their protection given "how dangerous the city has become." Comic relief included the guards insisting they'd never seen a well-dressed rat matching the description of Kenshin's missing pet, Mickey.
* **Breakfast and a bribe:** Escorted to a dockside restaurant, *The Witch's Arm* (*El Brazo de Bruja*), the pair stalled for time while trying to shake their minders. They bribed the waiter, Lucas, with gold coins smuggled to him inside a beer mug, arranging for him to pay local street kids to pelt the guards with rocks as a diversion.
* **The city hears the news:** While they waited, a newsboy ran past shouting that the maritime exits would soon be sealed over the "terrorist attacks," and that Mole had been brought down by a band of unusually powerful mages - including, by report, at least one human working alongside the Ashen. The whole city now knew a piece of what the party had done.
* **Slipping away:** The staged rock-throwing worked - the guards gave chase after the children, and Kenshin and Cael broke for the docks to find *The Aurora* and reunite with the rest of the group.

## Open Questions
* **Who is Naut, really?** He claims to care only for the Ilshiar people, holds a pendant with an unexplained magical link to Kenshin, and is chasing his own quest to find a sister and "a fragment of destiny" - how does any of this connect to the mysterious figure who sheltered the party at the end of the last session?
* **What is "the Casino"?** Naut used Daef's recovered document to sign the entire Ilshiar district over to this organization - who runs it, what do they actually want with the district, and how long can the ruse hold before the wronged noble finds out?
* **Will Erios's father survive the castle?** He was taken hostage specifically to flush out the "terrorist" - now that Erios is fleeing the city entirely, what happens to him?
* **Does the credit reach Erios's family safely**, or will fear of the guards keep the shopkeeper from honoring it?
* **Will the party make it out to sea before the docks are sealed**, and will Romina, Naut, and the tunnel group all actually converge with Kenshin and Cael at *The Aurora* in time?
